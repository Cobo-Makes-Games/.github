# Cobo Makes Games

Two people making [Mytheria](https://cobomakesgames.com/mytheria/), a pixel-art co-op adventure for one to four players, in Godot. Coming to Steam.

We write about how we make it on [the Cobo Makes Games blog](https://cobomakesgames.com/blog/), with real footage, real numbers and demos you can try. When a piece of the game is useful on its own, we put it here, MIT licensed:

| Add-on | What it does | The story behind it |
|---|---|---|
| [Shuffle SFX](https://github.com/Cobo-Makes-Games/godot-shuffle-sfx) | Sound effects you can hear a thousand times: shuffled variants, no back-to-back repeats, cooldowns and voice limits | [A pickup sound you can hear a thousand times](https://cobomakesgames.com/blog/pickup-sound/) |
| [LAN Discovery](https://github.com/Cobo-Makes-Games/godot-lan-discovery) | Find a friend's game on the same Wi-Fi, phone hotspots included | [Godot LAN multiplayer](https://cobomakesgames.com/blog/godot-lan-multiplayer/) |
| [Hold To Confirm](https://github.com/Cobo-Makes-Games/godot-hold-to-confirm) | A button for big decisions: hold it, or press twice | [Accessibility settings in games](https://cobomakesgames.com/blog/accessibility-settings-in-games/) |
| [Frame Trace](https://github.com/Cobo-Makes-Games/godot-frame-trace) | Find what makes your game stutter, frame by frame | [Finding a stutter with a trace](https://cobomakesgames.com/blog/godot-stutter-frame-trace/) |
